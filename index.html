Three convictions,
one ledger.

Everything on this page traces back to the same three commitments. Read them once — then hold us to them.

/ 01 — REAL ASSETS

Land before narrative

We start with what already exists: buildings, parcels, main streets, and the people who hold them. No abstractions — places you can stand on, measured in census tracts and assessed values, not in stories.

/ 02 — OPEN SOURCE

Capital in the open

The playbook is public: incentive maps, tract research, diligence notes, and formation doctrine — published, reusable, and attributable. Formation benefits compound when the method is shared, not hoarded.

/ 03 — PUBLIC BENEFIT

Benefit, by design

A public-benefit approach means the benefit is written into the work itself — jobs, local ownership, restored storefronts — not appended as marketing. Formation is separate from the benefit; both are stated plainly.
