Privacy
Notice

The short version: we collect the minimum you type into a form, we use it for exactly the thing you asked for, and you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

Only what you enter into a form on this site:

  • My Town requests — your full name, phone number, the town and state (or ZIP) you named, and your email address.
  • Guide-access invoice requests — your full name, invoice email, chosen guide or area, and an optional company or billing name.

We do not collect payment card details, Social Security numbers, background-check documents, or credentials — on this site, ever.

Why we collect it

  • To review your location and prepare (or decline) a local page.
  • To issue an invoice for guide-access services only after review and approval.
  • To email you about the specific request you made — nothing else.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or broker your personal details.
  • Add you to marketing lists you did not ask for.
  • Use your submission as an offer, an enrollment, or an expression of investment interest.
  • Share your details with third parties except processors who run our infrastructure under confidentiality terms (e.g., our database and email providers), or as required by law.

Retention & deletion

Requests are retained only as long as needed to answer them and keep honest records of review. You may ask for your record to be viewed, corrected, or deleted at any time by emailing reconstructionusa@proton.me. We confirm deletion in writing.

Analytics & cookies

This site uses lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics (aggregate page views) and no cross-site advertising cookies. Essential storage may hold your form draft and a local copy of an unsent request; clearing your browser data removes it.

Children

This site and its services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; submissions require an 18+ confirmation.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: reconstructionusa@proton.me.
