Place-Based Request

My Town

Start with the place you know. Tell us your town and state — or a ZIP Code — and we will prepare a local starting point for it.

What happens after you submit

We read every request

Your town is logged against the county and state you named — nothing is queued anonymously.

We review the location

Census tracts, county boundaries, and the state’s Opportunity Zone program are checked before anything is published.

Your local page is prepared

Reviewed locations receive a published reference point with official resources attached to it.

We email you

You will hear from us at the address you provide — when the page is ready, or if we need more detail.

Fine print, plainly

  • — A ZIP Code is not the same thing as an Opportunity Zone.
  • — Locations are reviewed before any local page is published.
  • — This request is not an offering, an investment recommendation, or tax, legal, or financial advice.
  • — We use your email address only to tell you when your local page is ready.
