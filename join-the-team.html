Why coordination, research, and agent development live here — and why we’re asking carefully, not loudly.

/ 01 — THE BEGINNING

Early adopters

The founding team was an early adopter of Manus, beginning work with the platform during its first week of public release — before we were operating under The Reconstruction Company name.

/ 02 — THE SHIFT

Executive function, not chat

As the work has grown, we have moved our central coordination, research, agent development, and team workflow into Manus. We need more of an executive-function environment and less of a chat-room experience.

/ 03 — THE STAGE

Scaling deliberately

We need a place where a serious working team can organize material, build and test local agents, preserve research, manage projects, and scale deliberately. Manus gives us a stronger environment for that next stage.

We look forward to building and scaling with Manus as Reconstruction USA grows.
